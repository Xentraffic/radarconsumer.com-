Dates to know

The dates below are the federal Marketplace schedule. They are not a complete list of every state exchange.

  1. November 1, 2026

    In most states, Open Enrollment starts. On HealthCare.gov, this is the first day to enroll in a plan for 2027 coverage.

  2. December 15, 2026

    In most states, this is the last day to enroll for coverage that starts January 1, 2027.

  3. January 15, 2027

    On HealthCare.gov, Open Enrollment ends. In most states, a plan selected from December 16 through January 15 starts February 1, 2027, once the first premium is paid.

Some state-based exchanges opened before November 1, 2026, or end on a day other than January 15, 2027. A licensed insurance agent can confirm the dates for a given state.
