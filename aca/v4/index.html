Self-employed

People who work for themselves often call to compare individual plan options for the year ahead. A licensed insurance agent can walk through networks and costs. This page does not collect that information.

Between jobs

A gap between jobs is one reason people compare plan options. A licensed insurance agent can explain what is available in a given area, and whether a special enrollment period may apply. Whether it applies depends on the facts of that situation.

New to the area

Moving can change which plans and provider networks are offered. A licensed insurance agent can review options for a specific location. Choosing this topic does not send a location anywhere.

Aging off a parent’s plan

Under current federal rules, a young adult can generally stay on a parent’s plan until age 26. People often call a licensed insurance agent to compare individual options around that time. The agent can also explain whether a special enrollment period may apply.
