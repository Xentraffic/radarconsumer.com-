Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This website (radarconsumers.com) is operated by XenTraffic Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you visit the site or call the number shown on it.

Information we collect on this website

This website has no forms and does not ask you to enter any personal information. We do not use advertising pixels or analytics scripts. The only third-party script on the site is from our call tracking provider, Ringba, which displays a tracking phone number (dynamic number insertion) so calls can be attributed to the page. To do this, Ringba may receive technical information about your visit, such as the page address (without query parameters), referring page, IP address, browser type and time of visit, and may use cookies or similar browser storage to keep the same number displayed during your visit. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs for security and operation.

Phone calls

When you call the number shown, the call is routed through our call tracking provider, Ringba. That provider records information such as your phone number, the time and length of the call and your general location (for example, area code or state) so the call can be connected and measured. Calls may be recorded for quality and compliance purposes. Your call is connected to a licensed insurance agent or agency, which handles any information you choose to share during the call under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use call information to connect your call, to measure advertising performance, to bill and reconcile with our partners, to prevent fraud and to meet legal and compliance obligations. We do not sell information collected on this website.

Sharing

Call information is shared with the call tracking provider and the licensed insurance agent or agency that receives your call, and as required by law.

Retention

We keep call records and recordings only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You can end a call at any time. To ask about, access or delete information related to your call, contact us at support@xentraffic.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under applicable law.
